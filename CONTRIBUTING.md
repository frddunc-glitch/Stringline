# Contributing to Stringline

Thanks for helping. Building, testing, the design documents, the fixtures policy and the licence rule for dependencies are all in [`docs/developer.md`](docs/developer.md).

Report problems and ideas on the [issue tracker](https://github.com/frddunc-glitch/Stringline/issues). By contributing, you agree that your contributions are licensed under GPL-3.0-or-later, the same as Stringline.
